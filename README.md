# Olai — downloads

**Latest version: 1.2.1**

- New install: download **[Olai-Setup-1.2.1.exe](https://github.com/thisissaivignesh/folio-pdf-releases/raw/main/Olai-Setup-1.2.1.exe)** and run it.
- Already installed: in Olai choose **Help → Check for updates**.

## What's new in 1.2.1
- **The Olai wordmark** now sits at the centre of the menu bar.
- **Print preview.** Printing now opens Olai's own print window with a live preview of exactly what will print, plus printer, pages (all, current or e.g. 1-3, 7), copies, orientation (automatic per page), fit to paper or actual size, colour or black and white, and whether to include highlights and notes.
- **Side panel tabs** show just a centred icon; the selected tab (and the one under the pointer) slides open to show its name.
- **Fixed:** laser marks sometimes left pieces on screen after a right-click.
- **Fixed:** page thumbnails were cut off and pushed to the left; they now show the whole page, centred, with its number.

---
These files are read by the app's updater: `latest.json` (what's newest), `folio-patch-*.zip` (quick update) and the full installer. The source code is kept privately.
